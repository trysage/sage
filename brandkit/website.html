02 — Frequently asked

Honest answers, plainly said.

No legal hedging. No "may," no "could," no asterisks. If we don't know something, we say so.

Q.01

Does Sage hold my funds or keys?

Never. Sage is a co-signer, not a custodian. Your keys stay yours. Sage only holds the right to approve or reject — never to initiate.

Q.02

What happens if Sage flags a transaction?

You get a Telegram alert with full context. You decide — approve or reject. Sage executes exactly what you say.

Q.03

What if I want a transaction to go through without Sage?

You can disable Sage Mode at any time and use your wallet as a standard Squads multisig. Full control is always yours.

Q.04

How does Sage know what's safe for me?

Sage builds a behavioral profile from your transaction history — amounts, recipients, timing and token patterns. Over time it gets more precise to you specifically, not just general risk signals.

Q.05

Is this only for technical users?

Not at all. Sign in with Google, connect Telegram and you are protected. No seed phrases. No configuration. No crypto knowledge required.
