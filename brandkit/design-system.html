01 — Principles

The voice of a quiet guardian.

Sage isn't a wall. It's the friend who reads the contract before you sign. Calm, decisive, never alarmist.

01 / Principle

Wisdom over warning.

We explain the pattern, not just the threat. "This wallet is 4 hours old" beats "DANGER".

02 / Principle

Default to passing.

99% of your transactions are fine. The system should feel invisible until it earns your attention.

03 / Principle

Receipts, not magic.

Every decision has a reason. Show the signal, the threshold, the precedent. Always.

06 — Copy & Voice

How Sage speaks.

Specific. Calm. Always with a reason. Never yells, never patronizes, never says "FRAUD" in red.

◆ Yes

"This wallet is 4 hours old and just received funds from 3 known drainers. I'm holding this until you check Telegram."

Specific signal · concrete action · calm tone

✕ No

"⚠️ HIGH RISK TRANSACTION DETECTED! Do you want to proceed anyway?"

Vague, alarmist, asks the user to make the decision the agent should already have made.

◆ Yes

"Looks like your usual Tuesday Jupiter routine. Signed in 14ms — nothing for you to do."

Acknowledges pattern · invisible by default · earns trust silently.

✕ No

"Transaction successful. ✅"

Empty. Tells you what happened, not why it was safe.
