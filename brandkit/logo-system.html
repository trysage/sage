03 — Rationale

Why Manrope.

Humanist. Calm. Trustworthy.

Manrope is the only candidate that reads as a person rather than a product. Its open apertures and gentle geometry match Sage's personality — an advisor who's read the contract, not an alarm system that shouts warnings. It renders cleanly at every size from a 16px status label to a 128px hero lockup, and its 300–800 weight range covers every Sage surface with one typeface.

  • Open apertures
  • 300–800 weight axis
  • Humanist x-height
  • Clean at 12px
  • Geometric calm
  • Variable font

06 — Don'ts

What breaks the mark.

Four things that are never acceptable, regardless of context.

✕ Never
Distort or skew

No stretching, squashing, or perspective transforms. The mark has fixed proportions.

✕ Never
Recolor the mark

Only Mint, Ink, or White. No gradients, brand-adjacent colors, or tints applied to the mark itself.

✕ Never
Add effects

No drop shadows, glows, outlines, blurs, or texture overlays on the mark or wordmark.

✕ Never
Low-contrast background

Never place a Mint mark on a green or dark-green surface. Contrast must exceed 4.5:1.
